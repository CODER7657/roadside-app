04 · Safety, security & privacy

Built safe from day one, not patched later.

Customers share their location and phone number while stranded, so trust is the product. Every protection below is part of the fixed price.

Only genuine apps can connect

Google Play Integrity checks block fake apps and bots from reaching your data or sending OTP spam.

Everyone sees only their own data

Customers can't see other bookings. Mechanics see the exact address only after accepting a job.

No cheating on job status

Arrival checked by GPS, work starts only with the customer's code, prices calculated by the server.

Location privacy

Live location is visible only during the job and auto-deleted within 24 hours; shared trip links expire.

Admin dashboard locked down

Only your allow-listed staff can sign in; ID proofs are visible only to them, and every admin action is logged.

Abuse & cost protection

OTP SMS limited to Indian numbers, booking limits per user, budget alerts on your cloud bill.

India DPDP Act 2023Consent screen in 3 languages, right to delete, grievance contact and a data-breach plan.
Google Play policiesTargets Android 16, Data Safety form, in-app + web account deletion, permission disclosures.
ReliabilityCrash reporting, error alerts, backups and an emergency "pause service" switch.

Money never passes through the app: customers pay mechanics directly by UPI, so no bank or card details are ever stored.
