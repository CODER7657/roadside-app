Without the app

Email [[GRIEVANCE_EMAIL]] from any address with the subject "Delete my account" and the mobile number you signed up with. To make sure the request is really yours, we'll call or text that number before deleting anything. We confirm by email when it's done.

What happens

Deleted within 30 days:

  • Your profile: name, mobile number, language, notification token
  • Your vehicles and emergency contacts
  • Chat messages and photos, live location data and trip-sharing links
  • For mechanics: profile and work photos, UPI details and, once no longer needed (see below), KYC documents

Kept, without your name or number:

  • Booking and payment records, for up to 3 years, because tax and consumer law require them. They're anonymised: they no longer identify you.
  • Records of admin actions and complaints linked to those bookings, for the same period, so disputes can be resolved.

Mechanics' KYC documents (ID proof, selfie with ID, address proof) are kept for up to 180 days after you leave, in case of a complaint about a job, and then deleted.
