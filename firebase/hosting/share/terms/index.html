1. What [[APP_NAME]] does

[[APP_NAME]] connects people who need roadside help with verified independent mechanics and workshops in our service areas (currently Ahmedabad, Ankleshwar and Bharuch). The repair is a service between the customer and the mechanic. We provide the booking platform, verify mechanics before they can accept jobs, show both sides the information they need, and help resolve complaints.

2. Who can use it

  • You must be 18 or older and able to agree to these terms.
  • One account per mobile number. Keep your phone and sign-in codes to yourself.
  • Mechanics may receive jobs only after we approve their profile and identity documents. The information they give must be true and kept up to date.

3. Bookings and prices

  • Before you book, the app shows a price range for your vehicle, problem and city. It is an estimate: the final amount depends on the work actually needed and on parts.
  • The mechanic enters the final amount when the job is done. If it is well outside the estimate, they must give a reason, which you will see.
  • The job starts only when you give the mechanic your start code in person. Don't share it before the mechanic has arrived.
  • Parts, fuel and towing are charged separately unless the price shows otherwise.

4. Payment

  • You pay the mechanic directly by UPI (or cash, if you both agree). The app shows the mechanic's UPI ID and the name on it. We never hold your money and never ask for bank, card or UPI PIN details.
  • After paying, tap "I have paid". The mechanic then confirms it. If they say it wasn't received, either of you can raise a dispute and our team will look into it.
  • We don't currently charge customers a platform fee. [[MECHANIC_FEE_TERMS]]

5. Cancelling

  • Customers can cancel at any time before the job starts. Please give a reason; the mechanic is told immediately.
  • A mechanic who can't make it before arriving can release the job, and we look for another mechanic. After arriving, a mechanic may cancel only with a reason.
  • Repeated cancellations or no-shows may lead to a mechanic account being suspended.

6. How to behave

  • Be respectful and honest. Don't harass, threaten or discriminate against anyone.
  • Don't ask for or pay extra outside the amount shown in the app.
  • Don't book fake jobs, use someone else's account, or interfere with the apps or their security.
  • Use the in-app chat and call buttons for the booking only.

We may suspend or block accounts that break these rules, after reviewing what happened. Blocked mechanics stop receiving jobs.
