[[APP_NAME]] ("we", "us") helps people whose car, bike, scooter or EV breaks down in Ahmedabad, Ankleshwar and Bharuch book a verified mechanic nearby. [[LEGAL_ENTITY]], [[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]], is the data fiduciary for the customer app, the mechanic app and the admin console. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules.

1. What we collect

If you book help (customer app)

  • Account: your mobile number (used to sign in with a one-time code), your name and your language.
  • Vehicles you add: type, brand, model, fuel and registration number.
  • Bookings: the problem, an optional description and photos, the pickup address, landmark and location, the price estimate, the final amount, and the payment status. We never ask for or store bank, card or UPI PIN details.
  • Location: your location only while you choose a pickup point and while a booking is active. It isn't collected in the background.
  • Emergency contacts (optional): up to three names and phone numbers you choose to add, used only for SOS and trip sharing. Please add only people who are happy for you to share their number with us.
  • Chat messages and photos you exchange with your mechanic, and any rating, review or complaint you submit.

If you work as a mechanic (mechanic app)

  • Account and profile: mobile number, name, profile photo, city, the services and vehicle types you handle.
  • Workshop mechanics: shop name, address and photo. Independent mechanics: the area you usually start from, years of experience, photos of your tools, and the vehicle and registration number you travel on (shown to customers so they recognise you).
  • Identity checks (KYC): a photo of your ID proof; independent mechanics also give a selfie holding the ID, an address proof and optionally a reference contact. Our team may note a short verification call.
  • Payment details: your UPI ID and the name on it, so customers can pay you directly.
  • Location: while you are online, to offer you nearby jobs, and during a job, to show the customer where you are. It is not collected when you are offline.
  • Work records: jobs, amounts, ratings and complaints.

From every device

  • A notification token, app version, crash reports and performance data, and a device-integrity check (Google Play Integrity / Firebase App Check) that protects the service from fake apps. Crash reports don't include your phone number, address or location.

We don't use advertising SDKs, we don't show ads, and we don't sell personal data.

3. Who sees it

  • The other side of a booking, only after a mechanic accepts. The customer sees the mechanic's name, photo, rating, phone, UPI ID and name, and (for independent mechanics) their travel vehicle; the mechanic sees the customer's name, phone and pickup address. Before accepting, a mechanic sees only the area, not the address.
  • People you share a trip link with: the mechanic's first name, a rough position, the arrival time and the status, until the job ends or 3 hours pass.
  • Your emergency contacts, only when you use SOS: your own phone opens a message for you to send.
  • Our admin team, to approve mechanics, resolve complaints and keep the service safe. ID documents are shown to them through links that expire within minutes. Every admin action is logged.
  • Service providers who process data for us: Google (Firebase: sign-in, database, file storage, notifications, crash reports; data stored in Mumbai, India) and our maps provider (map display and addresses). They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

5. Your rights

  • See and correct your details in the app (Profile and Settings), or ask us.
  • Withdraw consent at any time. Some features stop working without the data they need.
  • Delete your account in the app, or from the account deletion page. We delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, keeping only what the law requires.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer (below). If you're not satisfied with our reply, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Security

Data travels encrypted and is stored in Google's Mumbai region. Access is limited by strict rules: customers can't see other customers' bookings, mechanics can't change their own status or ratings, and ID documents are private. Admin sign-in needs Google 2-step verification. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board without delay.

7. Age

The apps are for people aged 18 and over. We ask you to confirm this when you sign up and don't knowingly collect data from children.
