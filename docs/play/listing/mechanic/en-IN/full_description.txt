For mechanics and workshops in Ahmedabad, Ankleshwar and Bharuch. [[APP_NAME]] Mechanic sends you roadside repair jobs from customers near you.

HOW IT WORKS
• Sign up with your mobile number as a workshop or an independent mechanic.
• Add your services, the vehicles you handle and your UPI ID, and upload your ID for verification.
• Once our team approves you, go online to receive jobs.
• A new job rings with the problem, the vehicle, the distance and the price range. Accept it or let it pass.
• Navigate to the customer. Your live location is shared with them only during the job.
• Ask the customer for the start code to begin, and add before and after photos.
• Enter the final amount. The customer pays you directly by UPI.

YOUR WORK, YOUR WAY
• Go online and offline whenever you want.
• See your jobs, earnings and ratings.
• Chat with and call the customer from the app.

FAIR AND SAFE
• Customers see the price range before they book, so there are no surprises.
• The start code confirms you're at the right job.
• Our support team helps with disputes.

Location is used only while you're online or on a job, never when you're offline.
